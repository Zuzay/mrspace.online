What we do

Most small shops need three things online and get them from three different people. Here it is one studio, a few doors down.

Build

A website made for your shop, not a template with your logo on it.

  • Design for phone and desktop
  • Online shop and bookings
  • Google Maps and local search

Shoot

Photos and short films of your shop, made by a film director, not a stock library.

  • Shoot day at the shop
  • Clips for Instagram and TikTok
  • Product photos

Care

After launch we keep the site safe, fresh and working, so you can stay with your customers.

  • Updates when you send them
  • Backups and security
  • A short report every month

Also from Mr. Space: tap cards and wristbands that send customers to your reviews and socials, commercials for local brands, and a space for artists to show their work.

How it goes

From the first coffee to launch day, in seven steps.

  1. Meet

    We talk at your shop and pick what you need.

  2. Shoot

    Photos and video of the shop and the work.

  3. Design

    You see the full site. Two rounds of changes.

  4. Build

    Pages, products, texts, bookings.

  5. Test

    You try everything before anyone else does.

  6. Train

    A short session on the everyday updates.

  7. Launch

    Live, and announced together.
