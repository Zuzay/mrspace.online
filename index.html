What we doSite · System · Care

The site is the part you see.

Most small businesses don't need a fancy website. They need one that works every day, and someone who keeps it working. The real value is the system behind the page and the care after launch.

The site what customers see

Clean, fast, made for phones first. Your products, your hours, your story, in your own look. No theme with your logo pasted on.

  • Design for phone and desktop
  • Online shop, bookings or both
  • Google listing and local search
  • Logo and business email setup

The system what you use

Your own admin panel. Add a product, change a price, print a label, see an order, without calling anyone.

  • Admin panel made for your shop
  • POS sync (Square and others)
  • Price labels, shipping labels, receipts
  • AI writing and image search, on your own accounts

The care what we do after

Backups, fixes, small changes and a log of everything we touched. Ask for a change from your panel; it is ready for approval, not stuck in an inbox.

  • Encrypted backups every night
  • Activity log with time spent
  • Reply within 12 hours
  • Usage limits and warnings for paid services
Made to measure

Written for your business. Not adapted from someone else's.

Every panel, form and tool is written from scratch around the way you sell, price, label and ship. No theme with your logo on it, no features you will never use getting in the way. The code is new for each client, and it fits like it was cut for you, because it was.

How it goesStandard delivery · 15 days

From first talk to launch.

Nothing starts before it is written down and signed. Fifteen days is our standard; it can be shorter or longer depending on what you need.

  1. Talk

    Fill in the short form. We talk about what you sell and how you work.

  2. Agreement

    Package, price, timeline and who owns what. In plain words.

  3. Contract

    Signed by both sides before any work.

  4. Payment

    Setup fee first. Then we start the clock.
