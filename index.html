How it goesScope · Build · Support

From first talk to launch.

Nothing starts before it is written down and signed. Fifteen days is our standard; it can be shorter or longer depending on what you need.

  1. Talk

    A short form, then a conversation about how you work.

  2. Agreement

    Package, price, timeline and who owns what.

  3. Contract

    Signed by both sides before any work.

  4. Payment

    Setup fee first. Then the clock starts.

  5. Build

    We enter your content and set up the system.

  6. Launch + care

    Live. Backups, fixes and changes from your panel.
