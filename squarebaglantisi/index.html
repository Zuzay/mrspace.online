Field guide / Square

Get your Square catalog ready.

Products, workshops and repair services need different handling. Use this repeatable guide to prepare the Square catalog for a Mr. Space website.

Never share your Square password, one-time sign-in code, payment details or API keys. Claude works from an exported file, not your Square account.

01 / BackupBefore editing

Start with a full export.

In Square Dashboard, open Items & services → Items → Item library → Actions → Export Library. Choose All items and Excel (.xlsx). Menu names can vary slightly by account.

Save the original untouched.

Make a second working copy for Claude. Do not filter the export unless you intentionally want to leave items out.

02 / CatalogThree item types

Keep three kinds of items separate.

Use Square categories for organization, and keep workshops and repair services out of the clothing list until their own website sections are ready.

01 / Retail

Products

Use categories such as Vintage Jeans, Vintage Jackets and Shirts. Make the garment type clear in the item name; keep size and color choices as variations.

02 / Event

Workshops

Use Square's Event item type when available and a Workshops category. Keep these hidden from the Rufcut product grid until a separate workshop section is ready.

03 / Service

Repair services

Use the Service item type when available and a Repairs category. Keep them out of the clothing product grid; the repair request flow will manage the service choices.

03 / SKU + SiteKeep data reliable

Protect the stock data.

Make each product easy to identify in Square, in the Rufcut site and on a printed barcode label.

  • One unique SKU per sellable variation.
  • Keep valid existing SKUs exactly as they are; treat them as text.
  • For blanks or duplicates, ask Claude to study the current pattern and propose options first.
  • After Square is connected, Mr. Space can assign unique SKUs to missing variations if you prefer.
Square categories do not publish items on the Mr. Space website by themselves.

In the customer panel, open Stock and use Show online or Hide online per item. Publish only selected products with confirmed names, real photos, prices and stock. Keep Workshops and Repairs hidden until their dedicated sections are ready.

Square Online channel settings are separate. Do not change them as part of this guide unless the owner explicitly wants that.

04 / ClaudeCopy and review

Copy, paste, review.

Download the Square export, make a working copy, and upload that copy to Claude. Paste the prompt below. Claude must report changes first and wait for approval before editing the working copy.

The prompt stays in English to match Square's field names. The page language follows your device where supported; change it from the menu above.

05 / Before importOwner approves

Check the changes yourself.

Claude prepares a proposal. The business owner reviews the file and imports it into Square only after approving each change.

  • Keep Square's original column names and order, and leave the Token column untouched.
  • Check that every sellable variation has a unique SKU.
  • Confirm that no price, stock count, size or event detail was guessed.
  • Keep workshop and repair items separate from retail clothing.
Import only the approved copy.

In Square Item Library, choose Actions → Import Library. Review Square's import preview before confirming. Start with a small, low-risk group if the catalog is large.
