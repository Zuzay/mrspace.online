Package 03 · Full system handover

Your system.
Your accounts.

A full handover puts your business in control of its code, domain, database, hosting and billing. We document access and transfer ownership together before support ends.

Handover checklistKeep this with your business records

Every key has an owner.

  1. Choose the owner

    Use an email address controlled by your business for account ownership and recovery. Confirm who will manage billing and access.

  2. Put the code in your GitHub organization

    The repository should belong to an organization your business controls. Give each person their own account and the smallest role they need.

  3. Keep the database and hosting in your name

    Place the Supabase project and hosting under an organization you control. Confirm the billing owner, recovery email and who can manage production settings.

  4. Keep the domain and email yours

    Your business should control the domain registrar, DNS, business email and payment methods. Record renewal dates and recovery contacts.

  5. Use separate access for every person

    Use unique passwords and multi-factor authentication. Invite a named collaborator for support instead of sharing passwords. Remove temporary access after handover.

  6. Write down recovery and restore steps

    Keep recovery codes in a secure password manager. Record project names, account owners, backup locations and how to restore the site in private business records.

A safer handover

No shared passwords.

We do not publish account credentials here or ask you to reuse your panel password. Account invitations and recovery details are handled privately with the account owner.
